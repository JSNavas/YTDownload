# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a tool for downloading music; nobody needs to have
a bad day over it.

## What's expected

- Assume good faith. Most friction is a language gap or a missing detail, not malice.
- Issues and pull requests in English or Spanish are equally welcome. Nobody gets
  corrected for imperfect English here.
- Critique the code, not the person. "This breaks on playlists over 100 items"
  is useful. "This is garbage" is not.
- Remember that everyone here is doing this in their spare time, for free.

## What's not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Discrimination of any kind, including
on grounds of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, ethnicity, nationality, age, religion, or experience level.
Publishing someone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that don't fit this
document, and may block repeat offenders from the repository.

To report a problem, open an issue if it's something that can be discussed
publicly, or contact a maintainer privately if it can't. Reports are handled
discreetly and in good faith.

---

If you'd like something more formal and detailed, the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is the standard in
this space, and its spirit is what this document is aiming at.
